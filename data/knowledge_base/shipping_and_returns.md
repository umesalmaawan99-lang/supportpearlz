# Shipping and Return Policies

## Shipping Methods & Timelines
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days. Free on orders over $50.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days ($15.00 flat rate).
- International Shipping: 7-14 business days. Rates vary by destination.

## Return & Refund Policy
Customers may return any unopened physical item within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Digital subscriptions are non-refundable after 14 days of activation. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item arrived damaged.
