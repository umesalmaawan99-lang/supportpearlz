# Product Pricing and Subscription Tiers

## Pearlz Basic Tier
- Price: $9.99 / month
- Includes: Standard feature access, email support, and 5GB cloud storage.

## Pearlz Pro Tier
- Price: $29.99 / month
- Includes: Advanced AI automation tools, priority 24/7 live chat support, and 100GB cloud storage.

## Pearlz Enterprise Tier
- Price: Custom pricing (starting at $299 / month)
- Includes: Dedicated account manager, custom integrations, SLA guarantees, and unlimited storage.
